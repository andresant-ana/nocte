## Terminology

- **RATIFIED:** explicitly chosen product requirement or direction, subject to formal change.
- **EXPERIMENTAL:** formal baseline for testing; not approved final gameplay/economy.
- **CANDIDATE:** possible mechanism or implementation, not a requirement.
- **DEFERRED:** documented but excluded from initial motor/MVP.
- **OPEN:** requires evidence/decision.
- **SUPERSEDED/REJECTED:** retain history, do not implement.

A doc may describe multiple statuses; always consult its tagged rules and [DECISION-REGISTER](DECISION-REGISTER.md). For discrepancies, registry and founder-approved amendments prevail; flag contradictions, never guess.
