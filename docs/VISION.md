# NOCTE — product vision and scope

## Product brief (founder-ratified)

Create a **browser-native** blackjack-inspired original strategy card game, with solo play and limited-capacity public/private multiplayer rooms, full-screen game presentation, Art Déco/noir worldbuilding, lively interactions, simple identity, persistent stats and fair competition. The game should be engaging enough to interest both blackjack fans and players seeking novel card-game systems.

**Primary values:** strategy over pure chance; easy onboarding with meaningful mastery; competitive comparability; social experiences; aesthetic excellence; responsible design without real-value bets.

This is **not MESA/21**. That project was deliberately discontinued after becoming too similar to ordinary blackjack.

## Audience and initial constraints

- First deployment is for creator and up to roughly **20 friends**; 20 is a planning estimate, **not** a service-level guarantee or exact concurrency requirement.
- Starting infrastructure budget is **R$0**, no subscriptions or money-spend required.
- Later public release/large scale/monetization are contingent and out of current scope.
- No forced premium account creation: play quickly, then recover and preserve identity when wanted.
- Browser game UX must not look like a regular information website.

## What success looks like (to validate)

- The first decision is immediately legible to blackjack players.
- Choosing a better strategy substantially affects outcomes over repeated matched opportunities.
- More than one viable approach survives serious adversarial testing.
- Human players report comprehension, satisfying decisions, surprise and desire to replay.
- Friends can host/join stable rooms without hardware setup.
- Audio/visual personality feels like a club, **without** hiding gameplay or inducing compulsive behavior.

## Monetization/ethics (ratified)

No real-money gambling, deposits, cash-outs, cash-equivalent rewards, purchasable chips, exchanges, tradeable balances or pay-to-win. All credits are fictional. A hypothetical future business model may investigate **cosmetic** purchases after explicit founder approval and legal/product review, never as a stealth addition to MVP.

Avoid compulsive engagement techniques (manipulative near-miss feedback, pressure notifications, unlimited farming disguised as rescue). Daily rescue is for accessibility, not extraction.

## Naming

**NOCTE** is the current favored **working** name; Latin *nocte* evokes nighttime. It has not been checked for legal uniqueness, trademarks, URLs or social handles. The product shouldn't make branding availability claims. The Art Déco/noir sensibility is approved independent of final name.

## No feature creep as substitute for skill

The founder chose **Duas Faces** from proposed directions. This commits to testing its viability, not permanently ratifying every knob and payout. Initial personal prototype feedback described the game as enjoyable **but still monotonous/close to blackjack**; this is useful qualitative direction, **not** a completed formal P2A human study.

Exact 5-card dealing, 3 Foco/Noite, Manobra/Leitura costs/eligibility and information timing are **candidates**, not finalized mechanics; [COFRE-FOCO.md](COFRE-FOCO.md) is the authority for the boundary. Contracts, Protocolos, achievements/economy and other complexity remain deferred until individual validation. No claim of live implementation.
